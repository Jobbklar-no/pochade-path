About Pochade Path

Field gear should help you paint—not become the project.

Pochade Path is being built for painters who want clear, practical guidance before they commit money and carrying capacity to an outdoor setup.

Why this site exists

Plein-air equipment is often discussed one product at a time. In practice, a pochade box depends on a tripod, head, release plate, painting surface, transport method, and the painter’s medium. A choice that looks light or inexpensive in isolation can become awkward once the missing parts are added.

Our goal is to make those dependencies visible. We plan to publish product reviews, comparisons, compatibility guides, packing systems, and maintenance articles for oil, watercolor, gouache, acrylic, and pastel painters working outside the studio.

Who it is for

The site is for beginners assembling a first field kit, studio painters moving outdoors, urban sketchers expanding into supported setups, traveling artists with strict luggage limits, and experienced painters looking for a more deliberate system.

Current status: Pochade Path is an editorial MVP. The review library is not yet published, and this site does not claim otherwise.

How independence works

Some future links may earn a commission. That commercial model will not decide which products are included, how evidence is described, or what conclusion is reached. Each commercial page will carry a nearby disclosure, and every review will state whether the product was physically tested.

We will not copy retailer descriptions, reproduce customer reviews or star ratings, or use product imagery without a valid license. Specifications will be attributed and dated. When information conflicts, the disagreement will be shown rather than smoothed over.

Corrections

Field equipment changes, listings merge, specifications drift, and manufacturers revise products without changing names. If you find a factual error, send the page URL, the statement in question, and a source through the contact page. Material corrections will be dated on the affected article.
