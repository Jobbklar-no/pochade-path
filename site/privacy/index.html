Privacy

A small static site with a deliberately small data footprint.

This policy describes the MVP as delivered. It must be updated before analytics, email collection, advertising, comments, or other third-party services are added.

Effective July 21, 2026

Data collected by this website

The delivered website has no account system, database, working form, analytics script, advertising script, cookie banner, or newsletter service. It does not intentionally set first-party cookies or store personal information in the browser.

Hosting logs

When the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical information such as IP address, browser, requested page, and timestamps for security, reliability, and service operation. That processing is governed by GitHub’s own terms and privacy statement.

Affiliate and external links

Future affiliate links may include identifiers that allow a retailer to attribute a qualifying purchase. Once you follow an external link, the destination’s privacy and cookie practices apply. A clear disclosure will be placed near each affiliate link.

Email

The email addresses in the current contact page are placeholders and must be replaced before launch. If you email Pochade Path after a real inbox is configured, the message, address, and metadata will be handled by that email provider and retained only as needed to respond, keep business records, or meet legal obligations.

Children

The site is intended for a general adult audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Future services

Before connecting analytics, a mailing list, a consent platform, embedded video, comments, advertising, or a form processor, the site owner must assess consent and disclosure requirements for the jurisdictions served and update this policy with the provider, purpose, data, retention, and user choices.

Your questions

Use the contact page for privacy questions after the placeholder inbox has been replaced. This policy is a practical starter, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
