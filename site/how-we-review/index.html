Editorial standard

Every conclusion should show what it rests on.

Our method separates hands-on testing from documentation, specifications, owner-reported experience, and editorial judgment.

Method version 1.0 · Published July 21, 2026

Five evidence labels

Hands-on testing

Direct use by the editorial team, with conditions, duration, configuration, and measurements recorded.

Documentation review

Manuals, warranty terms, manufacturer pages, compatibility charts, and dated technical documents.

Product specifications

Published dimensions, weight, materials, capacity, interfaces, and included components, attributed to a source.

Owner-reported patterns

Recurring themes across public owner discussions. These are summarized, never presented as our testing.

Editorial assessment

Our reasoned conclusion after weighing the available evidence, audience, tradeoffs, and uncertainty.

What we score

When a numerical score is useful, the default 100-point scorecard is: field stability 20, portability 20, setup and pack-down 15, adjustability and ergonomics 15, build and serviceability 15, system compatibility 10, and value 5. Category guides may adjust the criteria, but the weights will be published before the result.

Value is intentionally a small part of the score. Price can change daily; the physical and functional qualities of a product usually change less often. We do not display live Amazon prices unless they are supplied through an approved, policy-compliant tool.

Testing conditions

  • Record the complete carried weight, including required head, plate, adapters, and storage where relevant.
  • Use more than one working height and panel size when the product supports them.
  • Note terrain, wind, temperature, session length, medium, and cleanup needs.
  • Distinguish measured data from manufacturer claims.
  • Photograph or log failures, loosening, flex, wear, and inconvenient workarounds.

Comparison rules

Products are compared for a defined job, not ranked as universally “best.” A stable car-based oil setup and an ultralight watercolor kit solve different problems. Our conclusion will name the painter, constraints, and reasonable alternatives.

Affiliate and sample policy

Commission eligibility does not change the score. If a product is supplied, loaned, discounted, or sponsored, that relationship will be disclosed on the page. Brands do not receive approval rights over conclusions. Factual-check access, if offered, does not include control over opinion.

Fact-check and update policy

Each review template includes source links, testing status, reviewer, fact-check date, and last substantive update. Availability-only changes do not silently rewrite conclusions. Significant changes are recorded in an update note.

Important: No page may say “tested,” “we used,” or equivalent unless the test log exists. Desk research alone can support a useful guide, but it must be labeled honestly.
