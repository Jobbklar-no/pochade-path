Our review method

Evidence first, testing status always visible.

We will never imply that a product was physically tested when it was not.

See the full scorecard

  1. Define the painter and job

    Oil, watercolor, pastel, travel, car-based sessions, and hike-in setups need different equipment.

  2. Separate evidence types

    Hands-on observations, manufacturer specifications, documentation, and owner-reported patterns are labeled separately.

  3. Compare the complete setup

    A light box on a heavy tripod is not a light kit. We account for adapters, surfaces, storage, and cleanup.

  4. Show limits and dates

    We record what we could not verify and when specifications or availability were last checked.

Why use Pochade Path

Useful specificity, without false certainty.

  • Recommendations organized by painter, medium, travel mode, panel size, and budget.
  • Measured specifications preferred over vague labels such as “portable” or “professional.”
  • Clear disclosures near commercial links and a permanent site-wide affiliate statement.
  • No copied retailer descriptions, customer-review text, star ratings, or unlicensed product images.
  • Corrections and meaningful updates dated on the page.
